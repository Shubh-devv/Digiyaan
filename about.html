ABOUT DIGIYAAN360

A Kanpur-born agency that believes every local business deserves world-class marketing. No big-agency jargon, no empty promises — just results.

BUILT IN KANPUR,
BUILT FOR
KANPUR.

Digiyaan360 was founded with one mission: to give local businesses — salons, clinics, institutes, spice brands, banquet halls — the same quality of marketing that big brands in Delhi and Mumbai take for granted.

We saw too many Kanpur businesses being underserved: generic social media posts, no SEO, signboards that didn't reflect the quality inside. We built Digiyaan360 to change that.

Today we serve 200+ businesses and manage everything from Instagram accounts and video production to giant mall hoardings and LED signboards — all from our base in Kanpur.
